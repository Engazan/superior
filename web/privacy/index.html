How Superior handles your data, connects your devices and gives you control.

Who is responsible

Superior is maintained by Patrik Horvath. This policy covers the Superior website, desktop application, mobile companion and the official relay used to connect your devices.

For privacy questions or requests, email engazan.eu@icloud.com. If you use a separately operated relay or an AI provider, their handling of data is also governed by their own policies.

Your content and encryption

Terminal content, prompts, project information, chat messages and approvals are encrypted between your paired phone and desktop before they pass through the relay. The relay stores authentication hashes, not the pairing master secret, and does not decrypt that content.

The relay forwards encrypted packets while devices are connected; its application database does not store terminal or chat histories. It can still observe routing identifiers, connection activity and packet sizes. Hosting and network infrastructure necessarily handle IP addresses and request metadata.

Encryption does not hide information from your own devices or from an agent provider you ask to process it. When you send a prompt to an agent, its configured provider may receive the prompt, code and other context according to that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Permissions and other services

Camera: used when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. Superior does not upload camera images to the relay. You can paste pairing data instead.

Local storage: pairing credentials and mobile drafts use the operating system's secure storage through Expo SecureStore. Terminal and conversation content is displayed on your device; persistent conversation history is maintained on the desktop.

Updates: the mobile application is configured to obtain compatible updates through Expo's update service. Update requests are separate from the encrypted desktop relay. See Expo's privacy policy.

Website: this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the network request needed to serve them. See Google's privacy policy. Links to GitHub, app stores and other services open sites governed by their own policies.

Superior does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the mobile application or website. Operating-system diagnostics, hosting logs and third-party services are separate from this statement.

Keeping and removing data

Local settings and pairing records remain until you remove them. Message drafts remain until you clear or successfully send them. Desktop histories and project files remain under your control on the desktop.

  1. Revoke access: on the desktop, open Mobile access and revoke the phone. This removes that device's relay authorization and its pending invitation.
  2. Forget the desktop: in mobile Settings, open the desktop's menu and remove it. This deletes its saved pairing credentials and pending-operation references from the phone, but does not remotely revoke access.
  3. Clear drafts and histories: clear drafts before removing the pairing; remove conversations or other history on the desktop separately. Removing a pairing does not erase all drafts, project files or desktop history. Non-secret replay-protection reservations are retained to prevent reuse of old counters.

Do not rely on uninstalling the iOS app to erase all secure-storage entries: the operating system can retain Keychain data. Relay host registration records do not currently expire automatically. For removal of an official-relay host record or assistance removing residual data, contact engazan.eu@icloud.com. Never send your pairing secret.

Support correspondence is kept for handling the request and any necessary follow-up. Infrastructure log and backup retention is separate from the relay's application database; contact us for details or a deletion request.

Your choices and rights

You choose which desktop to pair, which permissions to grant and which information to send to agents. You can stop using the relay or revoke a device at any time.

Where applicable, we process data needed to provide the connection and support you request, and to protect the service from misuse. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, object to processing, request portability, or complain to your data-protection authority.

Send requests to engazan.eu@icloud.com. We may need to verify that you control the relevant device or host before changing records. Share identifiers only when requested privately; never share tokens, pairing JSON or QR codes.

This policy may change as Superior evolves. The date above identifies the current version.
